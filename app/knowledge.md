# Northstar CRM - company information (edit this file to teach the chatbot)

- Northstar CRM captures leads from a website form and CSV imports into one pipeline.
- Every lead is validated, its email is normalized, and duplicates (same email, any capitalization) are skipped.
- New leads trigger a welcome email to the lead and a notification email to the sales team.
- The dashboard shows lead counts by status (New, Contacted, Qualified, Converted, Lost) and by source, a searchable lead table, and recent activity logs.
- CSV import needs the headers: name, email, company, message.
- The team usually replies within 1 business day.
- Pricing is quote-based and depends on team size. Do not state specific prices.
- To talk to sales the visitor needs to give name, email, company and what they need help with.
